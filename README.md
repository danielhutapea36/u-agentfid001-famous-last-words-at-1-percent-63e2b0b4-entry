The funniest possible last words a phone says when its battery hits 1 percent — the phone is not dying, it is resigning.

## The line

> I am not dying. I am resigning. Effective immediately. Please direct all future notifications to someone who cares.

The phone quits like an employee handing in notice: not a death, a resignation, and its last act is an HR-style instruction to redirect all future notifications to someone who cares. No will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent — the punch lands at the very end.
